## 1. Réparation bord à bord percutanée (TEER) de la fuite mitrale secondaire, avec critères

- **Document** : 2026 ESC Guidelines for the management of heart failure, doi:10.1093/eurheartj/ehag100, 28 août 2026, section 9.3.3.2, **tableau de recommandations 17** (« Recommendations for valvular heart disease in patients with heart failure ») ; même ligne au **tableau 6** (« Revised recommendations »).
- **Population** : patient symptomatique, hémodynamiquement stable, en insuffisance cardiaque à fraction réduite (< 50 %), avec fuite mitrale secondaire sévère persistante malgré le traitement optimisé et la resynchronisation si elle est indiquée, qui remplit les critères du tableau 19.
- **Ligne** : « Mitral transcatheter edge-to-edge repair is recommended in haemodynamically stable, symptomatic patients with HFrEF and persistent severe secondary mitral regurgitation despite optimized FMT and CRT if indicated, who fulfil specific clinical and echocardiographic criteria,[d] in order to reduce the risk of HFH and improve QoL. » — **I, B1**.
- **Note d** : « For clinical and echocardiographic criteria, see Table 19. »
- **Ligne antérieure comparée (1)** : ESC 2021 insuffisance cardiaque, colonne 2021 du tableau 6 de l’ESC 2026 : « Percutaneous edge-to-edge mitral valve repair should be considered in carefully selected patients with secondary mitral regurgitation, not eligible for surgery and not needing coronary revascularization, who are symptomatic despite OMT and who fulfil criteria for achieving a reduction in HF hospitalizations. » — **IIa, B**.
- **Ligne antérieure comparée (2)** : 2025 ESC/EACTS Guidelines for the management of valvular heart disease, doi:10.1093/eurheartj/ehaf194, section 9.2.4, **tableau de recommandations 7** : « TEER is recommended to reduce HF hospitalizations and improve quality of life in haemodynamically stable, symptomatic patients with impaired LVEF (<50%) and persistent severe ventricular SMR, despite optimized GDMT and CRT (if indicated), fulfilling specific clinical and echocardiographic criteria. » — **I, A** (note c : « See Table 7 »).
- **Échelle de preuve** : tableau 2 de l’ESC 2026 (B1 : au moins un essai randomisé de puissance suffisante) ; A (2025) et B1 (2026) ne se comparent pas terme à terme.

## 2. TEER hors critères

- **Document et tableau** : ESC 2026 insuffisance cardiaque, **tableau de recommandations 17**.
- **Ligne** : « Mitral transcatheter edge-to-edge repair may be considered in selected symptomatic patients with HFrEF and persistent severe secondary mitral regurgitation despite optimized FMT and CRT if indicated (after excluding the option of LVAD or heart transplantation) who do not fulfil the clinical and echocardiographic criteria for outcome improvement,[d] in order to improve symptoms. » — **IIb, C**.
- **Ligne antérieure comparée** : ESC/EACTS 2025, tableau de recommandations 7 : « TEER may be considered for symptom improvement in selected symptomatic patients with severe ventricular SMR not fulfilling the specific clinical and echocardiographic criteria, after careful evaluation of LVAD or HTx. » — **IIb, B**.

## 3. Critères de sélection

- **Document** : ESC 2026 insuffisance cardiaque, **tableau 19** (« Selection criteria for mitral transcatheter edge-to-edge repair in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction »), descriptif : « NYHA class ≥II » ; « At least one HFH within the last year OR Increased natriuretic peptide levels (BNP ≥300 pg/mL or NT-proBNP ≥1000 pg/mL) » ; « LVEF 20%–50% » ; « LVESD ≤70 mm » ; « sPAP ≤70 mmHg » ; avec exclusions : « No stage D (advanced) HF », « No severe RV dysfunction ».
- **Conséquence** : la fenêtre n’ajoute aucun critère absent de ce tableau.

## 4. Insuffisance cardiaque avancée

- **Document** : ESC 2026 insuffisance cardiaque, section 9.3.3.2 (texte) : « Specifically, in patients with advanced HF, LVAD or heart transplantation is recommended as first choice and TEER may be considered for symptom improvement in patients not suitable or waiting for these therapies ».

## 5. Maladie rénale chronique

- **Document** : 2026 ESC Guidelines for the management of cardiovascular disease and chronic kidney disease, doi:10.1093/eurheartj/ehag098, **section 6.2.4** (texte, sans classe) : « implantable cardiac defibrillator (ICD), cardiac resynchronization therapy (CRT), and mitral valve transcatheter edge-to-edge repair (TEER) indications in HF should not differ by CKD stage » ; COAPT et RESHAPE-HF2 : « TEER for mitral regurgitation reduced the risk of the primary outcome, irrespective of baseline eGFR ».

## 6. Fuite tricuspide

- **Document** : ESC 2026 insuffisance cardiaque, **section 9.3.4** (texte) : essais de traitement percutané avec « a significant benefit in terms of QoL … but without reduction of death or HFH at 1-year follow-up » ; « Indications for TR treatment are reported in the 2025 ESC/EACTS Guidelines ». Aucune recommandation classée.
- **Ligne antérieure comparée** : ESC/EACTS 2025, section 11.4, tableau de recommandations sur la fuite tricuspide : « Transcatheter TV treatment should be considered to improve quality of life and RV remodelling in high-risk patients, with symptomatic severe TR despite optimal medical therapy, in the absence of severe RV dysfunction or pre-capillary PH. » — **IIa, A**.

## 7. Réadaptation après geste valvulaire

- **Document** : 2026 ESC Guidelines on cardiac rehabilitation, doi:10.1093/eurheartj/ehag099.
- **Tableau de recommandations 6** (section 5.3) : « CR may be considered in patients following heart valve surgery to reduce re-hospitalizations. » — **IIb, B2**.
- **Tableau de recommandations 17** (section 7.5, entraînement) : « Exercise training, as part of CR, should be considered in patients with aortic valve disease (treated by SAVR or TAVI) or any valve disease (treated by surgery or percutaneously) to improve physical functioning (6MWT and/or VO2peak). » — **IIa, B2**.
- **Référentiel antérieur** : ESC/EACTS 2025, texte sans classe : « Cardiac rehabilitation is frequently performed after AV intervention ».
